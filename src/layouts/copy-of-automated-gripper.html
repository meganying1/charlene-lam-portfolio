Modular Cutting Stand Fixtures

Designed innovative fixtures for Lyndra Therapeutic's cutting stand, enabling new R&D testing capabilities through initiative prototyping and testing.

skills: SOLIDWORKS, SLA/FDM 3-D printing, mechanical drawings, pneumatics

Background

As the name suggests, the Modular Cutting Stand was built so that it's parts could easily be swapped for quick R&D testing of cutting methods. I worked on exploring the modularity of three components: the feeding block, the hard stop, and the blade holder assembly
